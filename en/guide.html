AI Poster · 5 min

Pictures for AI Posters

Photo quality and angle directly affect the generated pigeon’s fidelity. Use sharp, unfiltered pictures of the real pigeon.

◉

Full pigeon

A well-lit full pigeon with head, body, wing, tail and feet visible. This is the main color and plumage reference.

Simple background · high resolution · sharp subject
◉

Side head

A strict horizontal side profile with the eye, cere and beak fully visible.

No front view · no tilted head
◎

Real eye

A centered close-up clearly showing the iris, pupil and natural contour.

No filter · no flash reflection
▣

Real ring

Photograph the complete ring with readable characters and visible edges.

No blur · no glare · complete number
Important

The generation should preserve the real pigeon’s identity. Blurry, dark or cropped pictures reduce accuracy.
