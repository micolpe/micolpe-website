Your pigeon loft, everywhere with you

Micolpe brings mobile and desktop together in an accessible tool designed to preserve fanciers’ data and simplify their daily work.

What is Micolpe?

Micolpe is a pigeon management application available on Android and Windows. It lets you manage pigeons, origins, pedigrees, matings, races, statistics and visual materials from one account.

The application is designed to keep working offline, then synchronize data when a connection is available.
