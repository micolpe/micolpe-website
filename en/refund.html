Refund Policy

The process for Micolpe access periods and AI Poster credits purchased through Paddle.

1. Scope

This Policy covers digital purchases of Micolpe access periods and Micolpe Poster Studio credits processed by Paddle. Paddle acts as Merchant of Record: it collects payment, issues the receipt, handles applicable taxes and processes refund requests.

This page complements the Paddle Refund Policy. If there is a conflict, Paddle’s policy and mandatory consumer rights prevail.

2. Statutory rights and request review

Your withdrawal, cancellation or refund rights depend on your country, the type of digital product and when access began. Nothing in this Policy limits rights that cannot legally be waived.

Outside an applicable legal right, Paddle may review a request submitted within 14 days of the transaction. Submission within this period does not automatically guarantee a refund. Requests are considered based on the circumstances, product use, credit consumption and Paddle’s rules.

3. Technical issue or defective product

If a material defect persistently prevents access to advertised features, first contact support@micolpe.com. Include the account email, platform, application version, Paddle reference and a clear description. Never send your full card number.

We will seek to fix the issue or restore access. If it cannot be resolved within a reasonable time, you may request a refund from Paddle. Defective or misdescribed products remain covered by applicable legal protections.

4. Micolpe access periods

When a full refund is granted for an access period, the related activation may be removed and the account may return to its earlier status. An authorized partial refund may result in a proportional adjustment of the remaining period.

Simply ceasing to use Micolpe, forgetting credentials, changing devices or temporarily lacking an Internet connection does not guarantee a refund when the Service remains compliant and accessible as described.

5. AI Poster credits

Credits are digital items delivered to the account after payment confirmation. Credits already consumed for delivered generations are generally non-refundable except where required by law or where the Service has a material defect. Unused credits may be considered when a request is reviewed.

When a generation fails before a result is delivered, Micolpe should normally not consume the credit. If a credit was deducted incorrectly, contact support so it can be checked and, where appropriate, restored.

6. How to request a refund

  1. Open the confirmation email or receipt sent by Paddle.
  2. Use the “View receipt,” “Manage order” or “Request refund” link.
  3. You may also visit paddle.net and locate the transaction using the purchase email.
  4. Describe the reason and provide useful information. For technical issues, also contact support@micolpe.com.

7. Refund processing

If approved, Paddle normally refunds the original payment method where possible. The time for the refund to appear depends on Paddle, your bank and the payment method. Related access or credits may be removed once the refund is granted.

8. Payment disputes

We encourage you to contact Paddle and Micolpe support before starting a dispute with your bank. This often makes it faster to check activation, the receipt or a technical issue. This does not limit your legal right to dispute a charge.

9. Fraud and abuse

A request may be refused where there is evidence of fraud, refund abuse, intentional consumption followed by an unjustified dispute or other manipulative behavior, subject to your legal rights.

10. Contact

For a technical or activation question: support@micolpe.com.
To request a refund for a Paddle transaction: paddle.net.

Keep your Paddle receipt: it contains the reference needed to quickly identify your transaction.
