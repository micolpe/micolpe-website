Refund Policy

Rules applying to Micolpe digital purchases processed by our payment provider Polar.

Publisher: Micolpe, MoroccoLast updated: August 16, 2026Lire en français
Request period14 days after purchase.
ProviderPayment processed by Polar.
Supportsupport@micolpe.com

1. Requesting a refund

Email support@micolpe.com within 14 days of purchase from the address used for the order. Include the Polar order number and the reason for your request.

2. Digital services already used

A request may be refused or adjusted when the access period has been substantially used or Poster credits have already been consumed, except where the Service is defective or mandatory consumer law provides otherwise.

3. Duplicate payment or delivery failure

A confirmed duplicate payment, or a paid order that cannot be applied to the account after verification, may be refunded. Contact us before placing the order again.

4. Processing

Accepted refunds are issued by Polar to the original payment method. The time needed to appear then depends on the bank or payment-method issuer.
