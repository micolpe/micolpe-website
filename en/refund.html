Refund Policy

The process for Micolpe access periods and Micolpe Poster Studio generations.

1. Scope

This Policy covers digital purchases of Micolpe access periods and Micolpe Poster Studio generations. It applies to purchases made directly from Micolpe and, when online payment is activated, alongside the terms of the provider clearly identified before purchase.

Nothing in this Policy limits mandatory consumer rights under applicable law.

2. Statutory rights and request review

Withdrawal, cancellation and refund rights depend on factors including the buyer’s country, the type of digital product and when access or performance of the service began.

Outside an applicable statutory right, a request sent within 14 days of the transaction may be reviewed based on the circumstances, use of the software and any generations already consumed. Sending a request within this period does not automatically guarantee approval.

3. Technical issue or defective product

If a material defect persistently prevents access to advertised features, contact support@micolpe.com. Include the account email, platform, application version, transaction reference and a clear description. Never send a full payment card number.

We will first seek to fix the issue or restore access. If it cannot be resolved within a reasonable time, an appropriate remedy, which may include a refund, will be considered in accordance with applicable rights.

4. Micolpe access periods

When a full refund is granted for an access period, the related activation may be removed and the account may return to its earlier status. An authorized partial refund may result in a proportional adjustment of the remaining period.

Simply ceasing to use Micolpe, forgetting credentials, changing devices or temporarily lacking an Internet connection does not guarantee a refund when the Service remains compliant and accessible as described.

5. AI Poster generations

Generation units are delivered digitally to the account. Units already used for delivered results are generally non-refundable except where required by law or where the Service has a material defect. Unused units may be considered when a request is reviewed.

When a generation fails before a result is delivered, Micolpe should normally not use a unit. If a unit was deducted incorrectly, contact support so it can be checked and, where appropriate, restored.

6. How to submit a request

  1. Email support@micolpe.com from the address linked to the Micolpe account.
  2. Provide the date, offer, amount, transaction reference and reason for the request.
  3. For a technical issue, include the platform, application version and information useful for diagnosis.
  4. When a future payment provider is used, also follow the instructions shown on its receipt or in its buyer terms.

7. Refund processing

If approved, a refund is normally sent to the original payment method where technically possible. The time to appear depends on the relevant provider, bank and payment method. Related access or generations may be removed after refund.

8. Payment disputes

We encourage you to contact support before starting a bank dispute so that activation, the transaction or a technical issue can be checked quickly. This does not limit any legal right to dispute a charge.

9. Fraud and abuse

A request may be refused where evidence shows fraud, refund abuse, intentional consumption followed by an unjustified dispute or other manipulative behavior, subject to applicable legal rights.

10. Contact

For a technical question, activation or refund request: support@micolpe.com.

Keep any confirmation or transaction reference so your request can be handled efficiently.
